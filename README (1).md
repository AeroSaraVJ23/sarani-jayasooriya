# Sarani Jayasooriya – Portfolio (Future Action Summit Australia)

A single-page, light-theme portfolio aligned with SDGs 4, 8, 9 and 12.

## 1. Add your photos
Put these files in the `images/` folder (any missing one shows a labelled placeholder on the page):

| File | Where it appears | Suggested content |
|---|---|---|
| `profile.jpg` | Hero (aircraft-window frame) | Portrait, 4:5 ratio, face centred |
| `aerogenz.jpg` | AeroGenZ card | Team / event photo, 16:10 |
| `an32b.jpg` | AN-32B engine card | Engine at the Faculty with students |
| `engex.jpg` | ENGEX 2025 card | AeroZone or drone show |
| `aero-girl.jpg` | The Aero Girl card | You presenting or teaching |

Keep each image under about 500 KB (resize at squoosh.app).

## 2. Edit the LinkedIn highlights
In `index.html`, search for `Highlight 1` to `Highlight 6` and replace each title with the real topic of that post.

## 3. Publish on GitHub Pages
1. Create a repository named `<your-username>.github.io` (or any name).
2. Upload `index.html`, `README.md` and the `images/` folder.
3. Go to Settings > Pages > Build and deployment > Source: "Deploy from a branch", branch `main`, folder `/ (root)`.
4. Your site goes live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/` within a minute or two.

## Notes
- The SkyT photos and drone image load from the SkyT team site. For long-term reliability, download them into `images/` and update the `src` links.
- Contact details (email, LinkedIn, GitHub) come from the SkyT team page. Check they are the ones you want to share.
